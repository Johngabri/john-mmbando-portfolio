What the figures suggest

These are review prompts based on the sample, not final explanations.

01 / OVERALL POSITION

Spend is below plan

Recorded expenditure is TZS 6.0M, or 10%, below the year-to-date plan. The figures show timing against plan; they do not establish a saving or a compliance issue.

02 / MAIN VARIANCES

Check outreach and supplies

These categories are TZS 5.7M below plan in total. Confirm activity timing, purchase commitments, delivery records, and whether the forecast needs updating.

03 / ABOVE PLAN

Review personnel spend

Personnel expenditure is TZS 0.6M above plan. Check the underlying period and classification, then record the explanation and any follow-up.

Evidence-led next step

Request an updated activity schedule and supporting delivery or commitment records. Review them alongside the plan, then document the explanation and revise the forecast if needed.

Method

Variance = actual expenditure minus planned expenditure. A negative value means spend is below plan.

Variance percentage = variance divided by plan. Year-to-date burn rate = recorded expenditure divided by the approved annual budget.

Sample calculations
Overall variance: TZS 54.0M − TZS 60.0M = −TZS 6.0M
Variance percentage: −TZS 6.0M ÷ TZS 60.0M = −10%
Actual burn rate: TZS 54.0M ÷ TZS 120.0M = 45%
