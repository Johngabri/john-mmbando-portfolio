SYNTHETIC BUDGET ANALYSIS DATA PACK

All amounts and document counts in this pack are fictional and were created for an independent portfolio demonstration. They are not employer, donor, partner, beneficiary, facility, payment, or programme records. They do not represent actual real-time updates or a real compliance finding.

Files
- budget-analysis-report.pdf: full analysis, interpretation, assumptions, and follow-up plan.
- budget-performance-sample.csv: category summary with year-to-date actuals, forecast, and supporting-record counts.
- budget-performance-monthly.csv: six months by category. It has no total rows; aggregate it by month or category to avoid double counting.
- budget-support-review.csv: fictional supporting-record completeness counts and follow-up values. Pending support does not by itself mean a cost is ineligible.
- budget-forecast-scenario.csv: simple second-half sensitivity cases, not probabilities, approvals, or recommendations to spend.

Definitions
Variance = actual less plan. Negative is below plan and positive is above plan. YTD budget utilization = YTD actual divided by annual budget. Year-end forecast = YTD actual plus the stated second-half forecast. Forecast variance = forecast less annual budget.

Review period: January-June, year intentionally unspecified. Currency: TZS.
