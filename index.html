01 · About me

Stewardship built on detail, analysis and trust.

A finance professional focused on making programme resources clear, accountable and useful for decision-making.

I am a grants management and donor compliance professional based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. I currently support the Afya Yangu Southern Program as a Regional Grants Officer with Deloitte Consulting Limited, bringing experience in partner accountability, financial monitoring, USAID compliance and programme closeout.

My work is grounded in careful review of budgets, funding requests and supporting documents. I have reviewed TZS 2.5 billion+ in partner accountabilities, completed 45+ field monitoring visits and supported training for more than 20 staff and implementing partners.

I hold a Bachelor of Accounting with Information Technology from the Institute of Finance Management, completed in June 2024. I am at the final level of NBAA Certified Public Accountant studies; the qualification is not yet completed.
