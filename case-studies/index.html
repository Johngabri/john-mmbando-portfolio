Case position

Partner support · illustrative loop

Turn a review question into a clearer next submission.

A constructive follow-up can resolve the current gap and help make future requests easier to review.

  1. Explain the gapPoint to the exact difference between the request and schedule.
  2. Request specific supportName the line, explanation or record needed to reconcile it.
  3. Verify and documentCheck any revision, retain a clear trail and record the outcome.
  4. Feed back the patternUse recurring issues as a topic for future partner coaching.

Scope: This is a general fictional example, not employer or donor guidance. Apply the relevant agreement, donor requirements and organisational review process.

i

Fictional case: TZS 42.0M, TZS 39.5M, and the TZS 2.5M difference are synthetic. The case demonstrates reconciliation judgment; it does not describe a real request or prescribe an employer's approval process.
